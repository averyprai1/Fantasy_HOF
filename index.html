Road to the Hall

Each bar tracks one requirement; green means it's met. Win % fills from .400 up to the .575 bar. Titles, top-3 finishes and playoff rate count completed seasons only; win % includes this season's games so far. Cards are ordered by HOF points.

Each card also shows the NFL head coach (3+ seasons) with the closest career profile. Both groups are ranked within themselves on titles, title rate, finals trips, playoff rate, win % and playoff win %, so a league-best manager lines up with a great coach; each coach is used once.

Career stats

Record and win % include championship-bracket playoff games (not consolation games). All-play is your regular-season record if you'd played every team every week, which strips out schedule luck. Click a header to sort.

How HOF points work

Points rank managers on the road to the Hall. Induction itself is decided by the four requirements above.

+4League championship. A single title isn't even halfway to induction. +1Runner-up finish. Second place is no longer highly rewarded. +1Playoff appearance. Baseline entry to the postseason. +1Regular-season points leader. Rewards true roster dominance over lucky playoff runs. −3Last-place finish (Sacko). One last-place finish erases a whole championship.
